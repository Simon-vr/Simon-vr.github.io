Study Path

  • There are quizzes in class, and everyone just used photo search to get by. So, I feel quite sorry for the teacher. (By the way, this seems to be the last time I used photo search, after that the AI era arrived, and Xiao Yuan Search Questions will no longer exist)
  • Whether it's Physics I or Physics II, it's very easy to prepare from 0 foundation in 3-4 weeks to get 90 points. Here's how (pay attention):
  • First pass: For the exercises assigned for each chapter, look at them with the answers, and whenever a formula is used, look back at that formula, and organize all the formulas that appear in the exercises on a piece of paper. (3 days)
  • Second pass: Go through the formulas on the formula paper: for each formula, find the closest example in the text to that formula, do it yourself, and understand the use of the formula. (1 week)
  • Third pass: Memorize all the formulas on the formula paper, mainly the meaning of each physical quantity, after all, it's a closed-book exam (2-3 days)
  • Fourth pass: Without looking at the formula paper, do the assigned exercises again, mark the ones you don't remember, and review them again before the exam. (1 week)
  • In this way, the results of both exams were quite good, although I only have a superficial understanding of the derivation and physical significance of the formulas, but it's enough to cope with the exams.
