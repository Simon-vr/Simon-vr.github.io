Basic Information

  • Course Start Time: Second semester of freshman year
  • Course Difficulty: 2.5/5, not difficult, the lab sessions are quite interesting
  • Exam Difficulty: 2.5/5, average, still need to review seriously
  • Textbook Evaluation
    • Digital Fundamentals (Tenth Edition): 3/5, a brick of a book. The content is clear and understandable, a qualified textbook.
    • Do not try to buy the English version just to show off. (There will always be people telling you that reading translations is not as good as reading the original... unless it's a very bad publisher, you can mostly ignore these words). You will regret it when reviewing, and you will never have the motivation to open this book again, and your poor English will not improve in any way.
  • Course Evaluation
    • Bian Jing, pretty good. It can be seen that she prepares for the class quite diligently.

Study Path

  • Attend lectures, do exercises, conduct your own experiments, take good notes, and there will be no problems. If you want to gain a deeper understanding of some content, you can look atThis teacherteaches, I think it's the best, completely from scratch, the slides are super detailed. Professor Wang Hong from Tsinghua University is also good, but there are some parts that are hard to keep up with, and it assumes you have a foundation in analog electronics.
  • The lab course requires simulation on Proteus and implementation on the experiment box. The end of the lab term seems to be related to sequential circuits. The final exam should be done quickly, remember that the faster you finish, the higher your score, don't dawdle with the simulation wiring, the grading is quite generous (from the perspective of a senior student, it's extremely generous).
  • The theoretical exam does not require memorizing any chip models (the most typical ones you will remember from the experiments), nor does it test the 555 timer (to be honest, I didn't quite understand this part either), the exam time is quite tight, the design questions remember to draw circuits and make a lot of corrections.
  • I had completely forgotten how the theory exam was, but as soon as I saw the materials from 2017, it was spot on, reviewing according to the focus of this exam paper is definitely the right approach.
  • P.S. Let me talk about my dark history; before studying this course, I impulsively bought TuringComplete at full price,not even during a discount, I really cried to deathLater, I found out it was just Proteus... I personally have reservations about this game, it's a bit boring, if you want to do it, just go directly to Proteus.
