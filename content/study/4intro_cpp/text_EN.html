Study Materials

  • Github Address
  • Theory: The syntax is tested in great detail, with various inheritances, derivations, templates, STL, and other miscellaneous topics all examined. There are no original problems from the usual exercises. It is recommended to take notes and summarize each theoretical problem in a timely manner, do not miss any corners in the exercises, and review your notes or redo the exercises before the exam, and there should be no major issues.
  • Experiments: The volume of problems is large, and without a competitive foundation, it should be almost impossible to finish. The first few problems mainly focus on C++ syntax, here you must pay attention to compile-time errors during regular practice, write and check them yourself, it's easy to be overconfident, and if you see compile-time errors on the exam and are confused, you're in trouble. The last two or three problems mainly test algorithms; if you have the spare capacity, you can try solving LeetCode middle/easy problems, but actually, if you can pass the syntax examination in front, the grade is already quite good. 24-year memory: The last two major problems were a bus system (bus, passengers, getting on and off stations) and a simulation of operating system process scheduling algorithms.
  • The mid-term exam lab questions for the 2021/22 academic year are not guaranteed to be accurate. However, other collected materials are even less reliable, so they are still quite suitable for practice before the exam.
